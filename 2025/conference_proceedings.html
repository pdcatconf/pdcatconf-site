PDCAT 2025 Conference Proceedings

The proceedings of the 26th International Conference on Parallel and Distributed Computing, Applications and Technologies (PDCAT 2025), held in Gold Coast, QLD, Australia, on 22-24 November 2025, have been published by Springer in the Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series, Volume 16465.

The full proceedings, including the table of contents and individual papers, are available through Springer Nature Link.
